JobSummit

Government jobs without the chaos. A free, calm Android app that shows only the jobs you can apply for, tracks every exam from form to result, and helps you practise for it. One clear next step at a time.

Jobs you are eligible for

  • New SSC, railway, bank, UPSC, state PSC, defence, teaching and PSU notifications, read from the official notices every few hours.
  • Only the posts that fit your age, category, qualification, subject and experience, with age relaxation worked out for you and the seats open to your category.
  • Your fee for each job, the last date, and the official link to apply. Share a job from WhatsApp or Telegram and the app finds it for you.

Every exam, from form to result

  • Watches the boards' notice pages and tells you when the admit card, exam date, answer key, result or a form-correction window is out.
  • One quiet morning summary of the forms closing soon, instead of a stream of alerts, and nothing at night.
  • Warns you when two exams fall on the same day. Adds dates to your calendar with reminders.
  • Score your response sheet: paste its link once the answer key is out and see your marks, section by section, with the exam's own marking.
  • Keeps your registration and roll numbers and portal passwords handy, and makes your photo and signature in each form's exact size.
  • Shows until which year your age lets you apply for each exam, and how many attempts you have left where they are counted.

Practice that feels like the exam

  • Timed tests in maths, reasoning, English, general awareness, current affairs, science, computer and banking.
  • Full mocks in the pattern of SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, IBPS and SBI PO and Clerk, RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP and more: same sections, questions, time and negative marking.
  • A Daily 10, the same ten questions for everyone each day, to keep a streak going.
  • Every answer explained. Questions you get wrong come back after 1, 3 and 7 days until you have them.
  • Weekly current affairs written from official PIB press releases, each with a link to its source.

Made to be easy on your mind

Designed for people who find job hunting overwhelming, including people with ADHD: the most urgent thing comes first, nothing important is hidden in menus, and small progress counts, with XP, levels and streaks for the steps that actually move you forward. Works offline once loaded. No ads, no tracking, and no account needed. To give everyone better job picks, it shares without your name which jobs people like you apply to; you can turn this off in Settings.

Google sign-in and backup

Signing in with Google is optional. It is used only to back up your app data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so it can be restored on a new phone. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files. Details are in the privacy policy.

Get the app

Free, for Android. Install it from the project's releases page; after that it updates itself over Wi-Fi.

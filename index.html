JobSummit

One clear next step. Less to keep in mind. A calm, ADHD-friendly Android app for Indian government and PSU jobs and exams.

What it does

  • Shows only the jobs you are eligible for, from your age, category, qualification and experience, including age relaxation and the seats open to you.
  • Puts your single most urgent next step at the top: an application deadline, an admit card, an exam day.
  • Tracks the exams you applied for and watches for admit cards, exam dates, answer keys and results.
  • Adds deadlines and exam dates to your phone's calendar with reminders.
  • Keeps your registration and roll numbers handy, and opens the official application page in one tap.

Google sign-in and backup

Signing in with Google is optional. It is used only to back up your app data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so it can be restored on a new phone. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files. Details are in the privacy policy.

Get the app

Android only, installed from the project's releases page. The app updates itself from there.
